Our Story

Agape Was Built in the Work—Not in a Boardroom

Agape began as a cleaning company serving short-term-rental properties. It did not take long to learn that keeping a property guest-ready required much more than cleaning. It required laundry, interior design, handyman services, supply management, restocking, maintenance coordination, and reliable people who understood hospitality.

We cleaned the properties. We managed the laundry.
We handled the restocking. We coordinated repairs.
We supported the hosts. We prepared each property for the next guest.
The work was connected. The systems were not.

That hands-on service model grew across multiple states and helped influence a more connected standard of property care. Along the way, we trained service professionals in white-glove techniques, strengthened property operations, and learned exactly where hosts, managers, and providers were losing time, money, visibility, and control.

Agape is the platform we needed while doing the work. Today, we are building one intelligent operating system where property-management companies, independent hosts, rental owners, and skilled service providers can coordinate jobs, verify quality, manage supplies, pay vendors faster, reduce platform costs, and grow their businesses.
